Moon Football

Delete your data

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This page explains how to delete, or ask us to delete, data associated with Moon Football (Android package com.christosgeorgoulakis.moonfootball), published by Christos Georgoulakis.

Moon Football has no accounts and no sign-up. It never asks for your name, email address or any other personal detail, so there is no profile to close. What follows covers everything the game does hold.

Delete everything on your device yourself

Uninstall the game. That immediately and permanently deletes all of it — your league season, results and standings, your squad line-up, rewards you have won, your sound setting and your advertising consent choice. None of it was ever uploaded, there is no cloud save, and no copy exists on any server.

No request is needed for this and nothing is kept behind.

Request deletion of analytics and advertising data

Some anonymous data is held by Google on our behalf. To have it deleted:

  1. Email chris.georgoulakis@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line “Moon Football — data deletion request”
  3. Tell us roughly when you played and on what device. Nothing we hold is linked to a name or an account, so this is what lets us find the right records.

We will confirm and action the request within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataOn requestOtherwise retained
Game progress on your device Not applicable — you delete it by uninstalling Until you uninstall
Analytics events (Google Analytics for Firebase) Deleted Per the retention period set on the Firebase project
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Deleted Up to 90 days, then removed automatically
Advertising data (Google AdMob) Deletion requested from Google on your behalf Per Google's own advertising retention policy

You can also act on advertising data yourself, without contacting us: reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings, under Google → Ads. And you can change your consent choice at any time inside the game, under Menu → Settings → Privacy Options.
